How this works and what to trust

First leg runs from the low before the burst up to the high of day when the pullback starts. Pullback is how much of that leg price has given back. The first leg must also be at least 0.4 of a daily ATR. Only the first trigger per stock per day is shown, and a trigger counts only until 12:00 ET.

Points (0–6) count how many of six conditions a setup meets: triggered within 45 minutes of the open, a first leg of 30 bars or fewer, a stop 1.2% or wider, a pullback of 32% or deeper, R:R of 2 or less, and a clean thrust (8 or fewer new highs while the first leg was building). Hover a score to see which it meets. In a replay of the last 34 sessions (447 triggers that passed the 5-day move rule), the share that hit target was about 8% at 0–1 points, 19% at 2–3, 26% at 4 and 42% at 5–6, and the order held in both halves of the sample. Higher points means more often right, not always more profit, because a close target pays less. For Ready and Cooling rows the points use the current price as the entry. I picked these after looking at the data, so expect live results to be weaker. About 170 other features were tested (relative strength, 5/15/30-minute and daily confluence, levels, premarket, market breadth) and none added much beyond these.

5-day move rule: a stock must have moved at least 3% up or down over the last 5 sessions to appear. In the replay, stocks that barely moved (within 3%) did clearly worse. I chose this after looking at the data, so the live track record is the real test.

Daily-chart flags are reasons (Quiet 40 days = no 6% day or 4% gap; Trails SPY = behind SPY by more than 8.6 points over 20 days; Below 200d = more than 8.8% under it) the setup tested worse: no 6% day or 4% gap in 40 sessions, a stall at the prior-day high, trailing SPY by more than 8.6 points over 20 days, more than 8.8% below the 200-day, or a prior close at its low. Flagged names are still listed so you can judge them yourself.

In earlier testing on about 16 sessions of 1-minute data, the full set of conditions on names with a daily ATR of 4.5% or more gave a small sample (15 trades) at roughly +0.6R per trade, and the wider version about +0.17R. These are small samples and need forward tracking. Long side only. Information only, not advice.
